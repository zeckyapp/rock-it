    Connect Zecky

    Server address for your phone:

    –
    1. Open Zecky Lock it or Zecky Pocket.
    2. Go to Settings, then Network, then Add a server.
    3. Name it, for example Rock it, and enter the address above.
    4. Tap Check, save and use.

    Use the number, not the name. Zecky needs your Umbrel's IP address, such as 192.168.1.20. Open this page through that address, or find it in your router or in Umbrel's settings.

    Away from home? Install Tailscale on your Umbrel and on your phone, then add a second server with your Umbrel's Tailscale address (it starts with 100.) and port 8137.

    The wallet port is only open inside your home network. Your router does not forward it, so it is not reachable from the internet.
